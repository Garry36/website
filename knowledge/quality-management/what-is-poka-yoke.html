What Is Poka-Yoke? Mistake-Proofing Techniques and Factory Examples

Poka-Yoke (Japanese for 'mistake-proofing') is an engineering design technique that renders it physically impossible for an operator to execute a process incorrectly.

The 2 Primary Types of Poka-Yoke Mechanisms

  • 1. Prevention / Control Mechanisms: Physical fixtures that mechanically block incorrect assembly. For example, asymmetric locating pins that allow a sheet-metal plate to fit into a stamping die in only one valid orientation.
  • 2. Detection / Warning Mechanisms: Electronic sensors, photo-eyes, or limit switches that detect an omitted step (such as a missing washer) and sound an alarm or disable downstream cycling until corrected.

Everyday Manufacturing Examples of Poka-Yoke

Proven Factory Poka-Yoke Solutions
  • Keyed Electrical Connectors: Molded plastic housings shaped so connectors cannot be plugged in reverse polarity.
  • Torque Verification Screwdrivers: Electric screwdrivers interlocked with a PLC that advance the assembly conveyor only after the exact programmed torque and screw count are confirmed.
  • Weight-Check Scales: Precision scales verifying that a completed carton contains exactly 50 stamped brackets; a carton with 49 units halts the line immediately.
