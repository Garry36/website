Why templates matter

Most ERP projects fail in configuration.

ERPrath starts from a working vertical template, so configuration is the exception — not the project.

Templates, not customisations

Each vertical ships with pre-built BOM structures, routing types, inspection plans and approval flows. Your team validates a working model instead of describing one to a consultant.

The right UOM from day one

Weight-to-piece conversion for fabrication, cavity-based output for moulding, batch and potency for pharma, serial and panel counts for electronics — enforced by the data model.

Statutory formats built in

GST e-invoicing, e-way bills, job-work challans, FSSAI and legal-metrology labels are native screens — not custom reports paid for after go-live.
