1. Zero Cross-Tenant Data Leaks

Every factory deployment operates on logical tenant isolation. Your plant's Bill of Materials (BOM), machine routing sequences, and cost registers are segregated at the database schema level. No competitor or unauthorized party can cross-query or view your proprietary production data.

2. Granular Shop-Floor Role-Based Access (RBAC)

Factories require strict boundary separation between the shop floor, the warehouse, and the executive suite:

  • Floor Operators: Can only update job cards, report piece counts, and view machine cycle times. Blind to vendor purchase prices and financial ledgers.
  • Storekeepers: Restricted to Goods Receipt Notes (GRN), physical bin movements, and stock issue registers.
  • Owners & Directors: Complete end-to-end panopticon view across cash collections, machine OEE, scrap percentages, and multi-warehouse balances.

3. Direct Statutory NIC Gateway Encryption

For Indian statutory GST e-invoicing and e-way bill generation, payloads are signed with asymmetric digital keys and transmitted over TLS 1.3 tunnels directly to the National Informatics Centre (NIC) gateway. Tax tokens and signing keys are stored in encrypted key vaults and never exposed to client-side browsers.

4. Security Audits & Vulnerability Disclosure

We perform automated weekly vulnerability scanning, static code analysis, and third-party penetration testing. If you are an enterprise client requiring a formal SOC 3 compliance report or wish to conduct a vendor cybersecurity evaluation, contact our security desk at security@erprath.com.
