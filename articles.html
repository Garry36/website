Domain 1 of 11 30 Core Principles

1. Production Planning & Scheduling

#001

Rough-Cut Capacity Planning (RCCP)

High-level validation checking if critical work centers can support the Master Production Schedule before running detailed MRP.

#002

Infinite Capacity Scheduling

Scheduling production orders purely based on delivery deadlines, ignoring machine and labor resource limits.

#003

Finite Capacity Scheduling (FCS)

Scheduling work orders strictly within actual physical machine hours and labor availability to avoid plant overload.

#004

Forward Scheduling

Scheduling operations forward in time starting from the earliest possible start date to establish the earliest completion date.

#005

Backward Scheduling

Scheduling production operations backward from the agreed customer delivery due date to find the latest valid start date.

#006

Master Production Schedule (MPS)

The operational plan specifying exact quantities and timelines for finished goods production.

#007

Production Activity Control (PAC)

The execution module tracking shop-floor queue times, machine runtimes, and material movement.

#008

Shop Floor Control (SFC)

Real-time tracking of work-in-progress, machine output, and labor hours at work stations.

#009

Dispatch List

A prioritized operational sequence generated for machine operators detailing which work orders to run next.

#010

Operation Splitting

Dividing a single large production run across multiple parallel machines to compress total cycle time.

#011

Operation Overlapping

Moving a completed transfer batch of semi-finished parts to the downstream operation before the entire batch finishes upstream.

#012

Gantt Scheduling

Interactive visual timeline scheduling for machine capacities, preventive maintenance windows, and work order sequences.

#013

Engineering Change Order (ECO)

Formal documentation authorizing modifications to parts, assemblies, or manufacturing drawings within the system.

#014

Engineering Change Notice (ECN)

System-wide notification informing procurement, stores, and shop floors of an approved engineering revision.

#015

Engineering Change Request (ECR)

Initial proposed modification to a product design submitted before engineering review and approval.

#016

Takt Time

The required production pace aligned directly with customer demand rate Available Time / Customer Demand.

#017

Cycle Time

The actual physical duration required to complete a specific operation on a work center.

#018

Lead Time

Total calendar time elapsed from initiating an order (purchase or production) to final delivery or stock receipt.

#019

Setup Time

Machine changeover time required to switch tooling, fixtures, or programs between different product runs.

#020

Queue Time

Waiting time spent by work-in-progress inventory before processing begins at a designated work center.

#021

Move Time

The standard transit duration required to transfer materials from one work station or bay to the next.

#022

Wait Time

Time an in-process batch spends idling after an operation completes before physical transport begins.

#023

Run Time

Active machining or assembly time required per unit or batch at a given work center.

#024

Throughput Time

Total duration an item takes to progress through the entire production line from raw material to finished product.

#025

Bottleneck Resource

The specific production resource or station with the lowest capacity that constrains total plant output.

#026

Theory of Constraints (TOC)

Management methodology focused on identifying, exploiting, and elevating the primary manufacturing bottleneck.

#027

Drum-Buffer-Rope (DBR)

Scheduling method where the bottleneck acts as the drum (pace), buffers protect throughput, and the rope controls shop floor release.

#028

Job Card / Traveler

Physical or digital shop-floor routing document traveling alongside parts, signed off at each workstation.

#029

Rework Order

A secondary work order generated to rectify defective or non-conforming parts to bring them to engineering specification.

#030

Teardown / Disassembly Order

Reversing an assembly structure in the ERP to reclaim components or raw materials into stock.

Domain 2 of 11 20 Core Principles

2. Bill of Materials (BOM) & Product Engineering

#031

Engineering BOM (EBOM)

The initial design-stage product structure organized by CAD functional hierarchy rather than manufacturing process.

#032

Manufacturing BOM (MBOM)

The production-oriented product structure reflecting the assembly sequence, consumables, and sub-assemblies.

#033

Service / Spare Parts BOM (SBOM)

Bill of materials mapping replaceable maintenance units and wear components supplied to end customers.

#034

Configurable BOM (CBOM)

Dynamic product architecture configured via rules and parameters to support configure-to-order (CTO) products.

#035

Phantom BOM / Pseudo Assembly

A logical sub-assembly grouping items in the database that are consumed immediately without being stocked.

#036

Indented BOM

A hierarchical report displaying multi-level assemblies with sub-components indented according to their parent-child depth.

#037

Single-Level BOM

A flat component list displaying only direct child parts of an assembly, excluding deeper nested levels.

#038

Flattened / Summarized BOM

A consolidated parts list aggregating total quantities across all nested levels for purchasing analysis.

#039

Where-Used Analysis

System search identifying every parent assembly or finished product that consumes a specific part number.

#040

BOM Versioning

Database revision tracking ensuring active work orders pull correct engineering revisions while archiving obsolete specs.

#041

Effective Date / Expiry Date

Validity dates assigned to BOM lines indicating when engineering revisions take effect or become obsolete.

#042

Component Scrap Factor

Configured percentage allowance on specific components to compensate for assembly breakage, crimp losses, or drops.

#043

Assembly Scrap Factor

Allowance applied at the parent level to compensate for complete units lost during final testing or integration.

#044

Co-Products

Two or more distinct, valuable products generated simultaneously from a single joint manufacturing process.

#045

By-Products

Incidental or minor secondary output generated during the primary manufacturing run (e.g., metal chips, chemical slag).

#046

Formula / Recipe Management

Process industry BOM architecture managing liquid or chemical batch ratios by percentage or weight.

#047

Unit of Measure (UOM) Conversion

Algorithmic conversion linking purchase units (e.g., metric tons), stocking units (kg), and issue units (grams).

#048

Dual UOM (Catch Weight)

Tracking an inventory item simultaneously by two units (e.g., steel plates tracked by sheet count and net weight).

#049

Alternate Parts / Substitution Rules

System-defined interchangeable components permitted when primary inventory lines run out of stock.

#050

Product Lifecycle Management (PLM) Integration

Bi-directional bridge passing CAD parameters, parts master, and revisions directly into the ERP.

Domain 3 of 11 35 Core Principles

3. Inventory Management, Warehousing & Material Flow

#051

Stock Keeping Unit (SKU)

Unique alphanumeric code assigned to identify distinct items, variants, and configurations in storage.

#052

Perpetual Inventory

Continual, real-time tracking of inventory balances updated instantly by every receipt, issue, or transfer transaction.

#053

Periodic Inventory

Traditional accounting method where inventory value is determined only by manual physical counts at specific intervals.

#054

First-In, First-Out (FIFO)

Valuation and picking method where older received stock is issued and costed before newer receipts.

#055

Last-In, First-Out (LIFO)

Valuation method assuming the most recently purchased stock is consumed first.

#056

Weighted Average Cost (WAC)

Valuation method recalculating unit item cost based on total inventory value divided by total on-hand volume.

#057

Moving Average Cost (MAC)

Inventory valuation adjusting dynamically with each inbound purchase receipt based on landing costs.

#058

Safety Stock

Buffer inventory held in stock to absorb variations in supplier lead times and unpredictable demand spikes.

#059

Reorder Point (ROP)

Calculated minimum stock level Lead Time Demand + Safety Stock triggering automatic replenishment orders.

#060

Min-Max Planning

Basic inventory control rule where stock below a minimum parameter triggers an order up to the configured maximum.

#061

Economic Order Quantity (EOQ)

Mathematical calculation determining the optimal purchase order quantity that minimizes holding and ordering costs.

#062

Cycle Counting

Continuous inventory audit strategy where rotating item classes are counted daily without shutting down operations.

#063

Wall-to-Wall Physical Count

Total shutdown inventory count auditing every bin and location across the entire facility simultaneously.

#064

Blind Receiving

Warehouse practice where receiving dock clerks count inbound goods without seeing expected quantities on the purchase order.

#065

Two-Step Material Transfer

Transfer workflow requiring an outbound issue transaction from the source followed by an inbound receipt at the destination.

#066

Direct Transfer

Immediate stock balance relocation between bins or warehouses executing in a single software transaction.

#067

Consignment Stock (Vendor-Managed)

Materials physically stored on-site by a vendor but owned by the vendor until consumed on a work order.

#068

Customer-Supplied Material

Free-issue inventory delivered by clients for contract manufacturing, tracked without adding to balance-sheet assets.

#069

Quarantine / Hold Location

A system-restricted virtual or physical warehouse zone preventing non-inspected materials from being issued.

#070

MRB (Material Review Board) Location

Warehouse holding zone for non-conforming items awaiting disposition decisions (rework, scrap, or return).

#071

Bonded Warehouse

Secure storage area where imported materials are held without payment of duties until entered into domestic commerce.

#072

Dead Stock / Obsolete Inventory

On-hand parts and materials showing zero consumption or demand over a defined calendar threshold.

#073

Slow-Moving Inventory

Stock lines showing consumption velocity far below safety stock and holding cost targets.

#074

Inventory Turns

Efficiency ratio measuring how many times total company inventory is consumed and replaced over an annual period.

#075

Days Sales of Inventory (DSI)

Average number of days required to turn total inventory holdings into revenue.

#076

Gross Margin Return on Investment (GMROI)

Inventory profitability metric evaluating gross profit generated for every dollar of stock investment.

#077

Pick List

Warehouse instruction listing parts, quantities, and bin locations to pull for staging work orders or shipments.

#078

Put-Away Rules

System logic routing inbound goods to specific warehouse racking bins based on weight, volume, or turnover frequency.

#079

Cross-Docking

Routing inbound vendor deliveries directly to outbound shipping staging or shop lines without intermediate warehouse binning.

#080

Wave Picking

Warehouse picking process grouping orders into distinct time windows to optimize forklift routes and consolidate trips.

#081

Zone Picking

Splitting orders so pickers handle only items located within their designated warehouse sector before consolidation.

#082

Kitting / Staging

Assembling all discrete parts required for a production job into dedicated bins before releasing the work order to the floor.

#083

Negative Inventory Allowance

System toggle permitting or blocking material issuance when on-hand inventory levels drop below zero.

#084

Kanban Card

Visual lean signaling tool (electronic or card) triggering replenishment of consumed components at shop bins.

#085

Two-Bin Kanban System

Physical inventory method where the emptying of an active bin signals automatic replenishment from a backup bin.

Domain 4 of 11 16 Core Principles

4. Quality Management & Traceability

#086

Certificate of Analysis (CoA)

Official document from a raw material supplier verifying chemical, metallurgical, and dimensional specs.

#087

Mill Test Certificate (MTC)

QA compliance document certified by raw material mills certifying chemical composition and mechanical properties of steel.

#088

First Article Inspection (FAI)

Formal dimensional and structural verification verifying the initial production unit before mass manufacturing begins.

#089

In-Process Quality Control (IPQC)

Stage-wise inspection checks executed at work centers during production to catch flaws early.

#090

Final Quality Inspection (FQI)

Final operational gate testing finished products against customer acceptance standards before packing.

#091

Acceptable Quality Limit (AQL)

Statistical standard defining the maximum defect percentage considered acceptable during sampling inspection.

#092

Sampling Plan (MIL-STD-105E / ISO 2859)

Standardized statistical tables dictating sample count sizes based on total batch volume and inspection level.

#093

Non-Conformance Report (NCR)

Formal quality record capturing deviations from engineering tolerances on incoming, in-process, or finished goods.

#094

Corrective and Preventive Action (CAPA)

Systematic investigation workflow designed to eliminate root causes of non-conformances.

#095

Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA)

Official system authorization permitting a customer to return defective or excess goods for credit or repair.

#096

Vendor Non-Conformance Report (VNCR)

QA document issued directly to a vendor documenting sub-standard material delivery and requesting debit notes.

#097

Heat Number / Melt Traceability

Primary raw material tracking code tracing steel, alloys, or plastics back to the specific furnace melt run.

#098

Parent-Child Lot Tracking

Traceability tree mapping which specific raw material lots were consumed into which sub-assembly and finished lots.

#099

End-to-End Traceability

Complete traceability path connecting vendor raw material lots, work center operators, and final customer serial numbers.

#100

Shelf-Life Management

Expiration tracking for perishable chemicals, adhesives, and coatings, enforcing oldest-stock-first rules.

#101

Calibration Management

Schedule module tracking calibration certificates, intervals, and lockout dates for shop floor gauges and instruments.

Domain 5 of 11 17 Core Principles

5. Procurement & Supply Chain Management

#102

Purchase Requisition (PR)

Internal request generated by MRP or department heads requesting purchasing agents to procure goods.

#103

Request for Quotation (RFQ)

Formal inquiry sent to multiple prospective vendors detailing specifications, delivery dates, and bidding terms.

#104

Vendor Quotation Analysis

ERP matrix comparing vendor bids across unit costs, payment terms, freight costs, and promised delivery dates.

#105

Purchase Order (PO)

Legally binding commercial contract issued to a vendor authorizing the purchase of specific materials at agreed rates.

#106

Blanket Purchase Order

Long-term procurement agreement with a supplier covering recurring deliveries over time under negotiated pricing.

#107

PO Release Schedule

Time-phased release quantities tied to a blanket purchase order dictating exact shipment arrival dates.

#108

Goods Receipt Note (GRN)

Warehouse warehouse entry verifying quantity, packaging condition, and receipt of goods against an open PO.

#109

Three-Way Matching

Accounts payable audit control comparing line quantities and costs across the PO, GRN, and Vendor Invoice before payment.

#110

Two-Way Matching

Simplified invoice approval comparing the vendor invoice directly against the purchase order (common for service contracts).

#111

Debit Note

Accounting document issued to a vendor charging them back for rejected materials, short shipments, or pricing discrepancies.

#112

Incoterms (EXW, FOB, CIF, DDP)

Standard trade terms defining transfer of financial risk, freight responsibility, and insurance between buyer and seller.

#113

Vendor Rating / Scorecard

Automated metrics evaluating supplier performance across on-time delivery (OTD), quality acceptance, and pricing compliance.

#114

Drop Shipping

Supply chain flow where the manufacturer or vendor ships ordered goods directly to the end customer on behalf of a distributor.

#115

Subcontracting Purchase Order

Specialized PO dispatching parent raw materials to an external job worker and purchasing back the processed item.

#116

Inter-Company Transfer

Movement of goods and corresponding billing transactions between different legal entities under the same parent ERP instance.

#117

Approved Vendor List (AVL)

Qualified supplier registry restricting purchasing agents from procuring critical parts from unvetted vendors.

#118

Landed Cost Allocation

Mathematical distribution of freight, customs, and port charges across purchased line items based on weight, volume, or cost.

Domain 6 of 11 11 Core Principles

6. Sales, Order Management & Delivery

#119

Available-to-Promise (ATP)

Uncommitted inventory and planned production calculated to give customers accurate, firm delivery commitments.

#120

Capable-to-Promise (CTP)

Advanced scheduling check that determines delivery dates by factoring in idle machine capacity and raw material lead times.

#121

Sales Order (SO)

Internal sales confirmation document confirming customer orders, specs, pricing, and agreed delivery dates.

#122

Proforma Invoice

Preliminary commercial invoice sent to a buyer prior to shipment to arrange financing, letters of credit, or advance payments.

#123

Delivery Challan / Packing Slip

Shipping transit document accompanying goods in transit, listing physical package weights and serial numbers.

#124

Backorder

Unfulfilled sales order volume caused by stock deficits, queued for delivery as soon as production or procurement lands.

#125

Order-to-Cash (O2C)

Complete macro business process spanning customer inquiry, quotation, order processing, delivery, invoicing, and cash collection.

#126

Credit Limit Check

Automatic financial lock freezing customer sales order processing if invoices exceed approved credit limits or terms.

#127

Sales Commission Engine

Module calculating sales representative commissions based on margin percentages, booking value, or cash collections.

#128

Customer Consignment

Shipping finished inventory to customer premises while retaining ownership until the customer withdraws it for use.

#129

Price Lists & Discount Matrices

Dynamic pricing engines supporting tier discounts, volume breaks, and customer-group currency pricing.

Domain 7 of 11 19 Core Principles

7. Cost Accounting & Financial Management

#130

Standard Costing

Pre-calculated manufacturing cost assignment based on engineering BOM quantities, standard labor hours, and expected overhead.

#131

Actual Costing

Retrospective manufacturing accounting allocating real-time material purchase prices, scrap, and actual logged labor hours.

#132

Purchase Price Variance (PPV)

Financial variance measuring the difference between standard baseline cost and actual purchase price paid Actual Cost − Standard Cost.

#133

Material Usage Variance (MUV)

Cost difference measuring whether more or less raw material was consumed in production than specified in the BOM.

#134

Labor Rate Variance (LRV)

Variance measuring deviations between standard hourly labor rates and actual compensation rates paid.

#135

Labor Efficiency Variance (LEV)

Variance measuring whether operations took more or fewer labor hours than budgeted in the master routing.

#136

Overhead Absorption / Recovery

Accounting process allocating factory indirect costs (rent, power, depreciation) to manufactured units using driver rates.

#137

Cost Center

An internal department or division (e.g., Tool Room, QA Lab) that incurs expenses but does not generate direct revenue.

#138

Profit Center

A strategic operational unit or product line responsible for generating its own revenue streams and managing expenses.

#139

General Ledger (GL)

Central accounting registry tracking all debit and credit postings organized by the company's Chart of Accounts.

#140

Accounts Payable (AP)

Accounting module tracking short-term liabilities owed to vendors and suppliers for materials and services.

#141

Accounts Receivable (AR)

Accounting module tracking incoming cash owed by customers for delivered goods and completed projects.

#142

Reconciliation

Financial audit process verifying that sub-ledgers (inventory, AP, AR) match balance sheet totals and bank records.

#143

Depreciation Schedule

Amortization tables calculating periodic asset value deductions for capital equipment based on useful lifespan.

#144

Work-in-Progress (WIP) Valuation

Balance-sheet asset valuation capturing capital tied up in unfinished jobs on the factory floor.

#145

Cost of Goods Sold (COGS)

Total direct material, direct labor, and factory overhead expenses incurred to produce sold goods.

#146

Activity-Based Costing (ABC Costing)

Costing model assigning overhead expenses to products based on actual activities consumed (e.g., setups, inspections).

#147

Month-End Close

Operational accounting procedure reconciling accounts, locking transaction dates, and generating standard financial statements.

#148

Fiscal Calendar

Accounting year structure defining financial quarters, periods, and audit dates distinct from the standard calendar year.

Domain 8 of 11 10 Core Principles

8. Maintenance, Repair & Operations (MRO)

#149

Computerized Maintenance Management System (CMMS)

Integrated module managing machine upkeep, equipment maintenance logs, and spare parts.

#150

Preventive Maintenance (PM)

Scheduled, routine servicing executed on plant machinery based on elapsed operating hours or calendar intervals.

#151

Predictive Maintenance (PdM)

Condition-based maintenance triggered by real-time IoT sensors tracking vibration, temperature, and lubrication oil quality.

#152

Corrective / Breakdown Maintenance

Unplanned repair work orders initiated immediately upon machine failure or sudden mechanical breakdown.

#153

Mean Time Between Failures (MTBF)

Reliability metric measuring average operational hours elapsed between machine breakdown events.

#154

Mean Time to Repair (MTTR)

Maintenance efficiency metric measuring average time required to troubleshoot, fix, and restart failed equipment.

#155

Equipment Master / Asset Register

Central database logging machine specs, serial numbers, warranty terms, installation dates, and maintenance logs.

#156

MRO Spare Parts Inventory

Managing maintenance parts (bearings, seals, lubricants, hydraulic filters) with min-max reordering logic.

#157

Lockout/Tagout (LOTO)

Safety compliance workflow in the ERP tracking hazardous energy isolation before equipment maintenance begins.

#158

Total Productive Maintenance (TPM)

Lean maintenance strategy integrating machine operators directly into routine daily cleaning and inspection tasks.

Domain 9 of 11 14 Core Principles

9. Manufacturing Execution System (MES) & Plant Floor Operations

#159

Overall Equipment Effectiveness (OEE)

Benchmark metric measuring equipment productivity calculated as Availability × Performance × Quality.

#160

Availability Rate

The ratio of actual equipment operating runtime compared against scheduled operating shift hours.

#161

Performance Rate

The ratio measuring machine operating speed compared against its designed nameplate speed.

#162

Quality Rate

The ratio of usable, acceptable parts produced compared against total parts run (including scrap and rework).

#163

Human-Machine Interface (HMI)

Digital touchscreen panels mounted at work centers allowing operators to view digital work orders and input scrap.

#164

Supervisory Control and Data Acquisition (SCADA)

Industrial automation control system gathering real-time telemetry from shop floor PLCs.

#165

Programmable Logic Controller (PLC)

Ruggedized industrial computers directly controlling motors, sensors, and valves, streaming output counts to ERP.

#166

Andon Board

Shop-floor visual notification display showing real-time production status, output alerts, and maintenance alarms.

#167

Barcode / 2D DataMatrix Scanning

Rapid optical data capture used to issue inventory, clock labor operations, and record lot movements.

#168

Radio-Frequency Identification (RFID)

Wireless tracking using electromagnetic tags to monitor asset and pallet locations through facility portals.

#169

Edge Gateway

Hardware connecting industrial machine protocols (e.g., OPC-UA, Modbus) to cloud or on-premise ERP databases.

#170

Digital Work Instructions

Interactive, paperless setup guides and 3D assembly models displayed directly to shop floor assemblers.

#171

Labor Clocking / Time Tracking

Digital system capture recording operator punch times against specific work order operational phases.

#172

Downtime Reason Codes

Standardized categorizations logged by operators when machines halt (e.g., tool breakage, material stockout, power failure).

Domain 10 of 11 13 Core Principles

10. Manufacturing Strategies & Business Models

#173

Make-to-Stock (MTS)

Manufacturing model where finished goods are produced in advance based on sales forecasts and stocked in warehouses.

#174

Make-to-Order (MTO)

Production approach where work order creation begins only after receiving a confirmed customer purchase order.

#175

Assemble-to-Order (ATO)

Strategy where standard sub-assemblies are stocked in advance and configured rapidly upon order arrival.

#176

Engineer-to-Order (ETO)

Heavy custom fabrication where engineering design, BOM creation, and specialized procurement occur per customer order.

#177

Configure-to-Order (CTO)

Standard base platforms built with custom options selected by customers via configured BOM rules.

#178

Design-to-Order (DTO)

Similar to ETO, involving complete ground-up structural engineering and prototype development before manufacturing.

#179

Continuous / Process Manufacturing

Non-stop formula manufacturing producing fluids, gases, or powders (e.g., refining, chemical synthesis).

#180

Discrete Manufacturing

Production of distinct, identifiable physical units assembled from bills of materials (e.g., valves, pumps, gearboxes).

#181

Batch Manufacturing

Producing goods in discrete, identifiable production lot quantities sharing identical setup and formulation parameters.

#182

Repetitive Manufacturing

High-volume, continuous assembly line production of identical items running continuously over extended timeframes.

#183

Job Shop

Highly flexible manufacturing environment handling small batches of custom, non-standard parts using general-purpose machinery.

#184

Remanufacturing / Core Exchange

Rebuilding used mechanical assemblies to original performance specs and crediting customer core units.

#185

Toll Manufacturing

Contract arrangement where a client supplies raw materials and pays a fee for processing services on client-owned stock.

Domain 11 of 11 15 Core Principles

11. System Architecture, Data & Governance

#186

Master Data Management (MDM)

Centralized governance ensuring critical entity data (items, vendors, customers, accounts) remains accurate and clean.

#187

Chart of Accounts (CoA)

Structured index of every general ledger account code used to categorize corporate financial transactions.

#188

Enterprise Application Integration (EAI)

Software architecture middleware synchronizing data across the ERP, CRM, PLM, and WMS platforms.

#189

Application Programming Interface (API)

Structured software endpoints (e.g., REST, GraphQL) enabling third-party tools to interact with ERP records.

#190

Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)

Security policy assigning user permissions and access rights based on specific job responsibilities.

#191

Audit Trail

Immutable logging database recording user timestamps, prior values, and edited parameters for every data change.

#192

Electronic Data Interchange (EDI)

Standardized, paperless B2B communication protocol transmitting POs, ASNs, and invoices between trade partners.

#193

Advance Shipping Notice (ASN / EDI 856)

Electronic notification sent by a supplier informing the buyer of an inbound delivery's exact pallet contents.

#194

Data Migration

Extracting, cleaning, transforming, and importing legacy database records into a target production ERP environment.

#195

Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) ERP

Multi-tenant cloud ERP delivered over the web and maintained entirely by the software vendor.

#196

On-Premise ERP

Self-hosted ERP deployment where database servers and maintenance remain within corporate data centers.

#197

Hybrid ERP / Two-Tier ERP

Corporate architecture running an enterprise tier at headquarters while operating lighter ERP platforms at manufacturing plants.

#198

Business Intelligence (BI) Dashboard

Real-time visual reporting tools tracking operational KPIs like inventory turns, OEE, and gross margins.

#199

Database Concurrency Control

Mechanism ensuring multiple concurrent users do not overwrite the same inventory ledger records simultaneously.

#200

Disaster Recovery Plan (DRP)

Failover infrastructure, automated database backup policies, and business continuity plans protecting against system downtime.
